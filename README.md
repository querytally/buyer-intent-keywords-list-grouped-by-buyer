# Buyer Intent Keywords List, Grouped by Buyer

A buyer intent keywords list holds the searches people type when they are ready to hire, pay or choose, not just to read. This one groups the phrase patterns by what the buyer does next: hire, check the price, or compare.

Swap in your own service and town, keep each group on one page, and drop any line a real customer of yours would never type.

## How to use it

1. Replace `[service]` with what you sell, `[job]` with one task you do and `[city]` with where you work.
2. Keep the lines of one group together. They are wordings of one need, so they share one page.
3. Read each line out loud as your customer would. If it sounds wrong for your business, delete it.

The examples below use one trade: say a garage door repair company in Phoenix.

## Hire: ready to book

| Pattern | Example | Page it needs |
|---|---|---|
| `[service] near me` | garage door repair near me | Service page, phone and hours at the top |
| `[service] [city]` | garage door repair phoenix | Service page that names the area |
| `emergency [service]` | emergency garage door repair | Same-day page, phone first |
| `same day [service]` | same day garage door repair | Same-day page |
| `[job] [service]` | broken spring garage door repair | One page per job type |
| `[service] company [city]` | garage door company phoenix | Service page with photos of real jobs |

## Price: checking the cost

| Pattern | Example | Page it needs |
|---|---|---|
| `[job] cost` | garage door spring replacement cost | Price page with honest ranges |
| `[job] price` | garage door opener installation price | Price page |
| `[service] quote` | garage door repair quote | Short quote form |
| `how much is [job]` | how much is a new garage door | Price page, the answer in the first line |
| `affordable [service]` | affordable garage door repair | Price page that names the cheapest option |

## Compare: choosing who, or which

| Pattern | Example | Page it needs |
|---|---|---|
| `best [service] [city]` | best garage door company phoenix | Proof page: reviews, years, warranty |
| `[service] reviews` | garage door repair reviews | Reviews page |
| `[option a] vs [option b]` | belt drive vs chain drive opener | Comparison page |
| `[product brand] [service]` | (a brand you install) opener repair | One page per brand you service |
| `licensed [service] [city]` | licensed garage door contractor phoenix | Trust page with licence details |

## Why small pools are normal

Most lines above draw small monthly counts. That is expected. The more exactly a phrase names a job and a place, the fewer people type it, and the more of them mean to buy.

So judge a group by what one customer is worth, not by the count alone. A small group whose buyers each pay for a new door can beat a large phrase full of people who only want to read.

For how to read buyer groups and which competitors already have a page for each, [Querytally](https://querytally.com/search-intent/buyer-intent-keywords/) has the longer guide.
